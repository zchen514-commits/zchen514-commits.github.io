My name is Zetong Chen, and I am a Pre-Business undergraduate at the University of California, Riverside. As an international student from China, I am learning to work across languages, cultures, and different expectations. I am interested in business because it connects people, ideas, and practical decisions. Marketing and communication especially interest me because they influence how people understand a product, a service, or an organization. My current goal is to build a strong foundation in business while improving the communication and digital skills that will help me contribute to future projects and professional opportunities.

My college experience has helped me understand that learning involves more than completing assignments. Writing courses have encouraged me to explain my ideas clearly, organize information, and revise my work after receiving feedback. Economics has introduced me to questions about incentives, prices, markets, and everyday choices. Other coursework has helped me think about social issues and consider perspectives different from my own. I am still exploring the areas of business that fit my strengths, but I enjoy connecting classroom topics with situations I see in daily life. I want to become a person who can explain both an idea and the reasons behind it.

I value teamwork, steady improvement, and responsibility. Basketball is an important part of my life because it teaches me to communicate, stay focused, and keep practicing when progress is slow. I also have a background in Latin dance, which has helped me develop discipline and confidence through repeated practice. These activities remind me that success usually comes from small improvements over time. In group assignments, I want to listen carefully, understand the goal, and contribute work that helps the whole group. I am continuing to develop the confidence to speak up and share my ideas more effectively.

Studying in English has also shaped my approach to communication. I try to use clear language and check whether my message is easy to understand. Moving between Chinese and English makes me more aware of how tone, word choice, and context affect a conversation. This experience connects with my interest in marketing because an effective message needs to match its audience. I enjoy opportunities to practice writing, presenting, and explaining information. I also use learning resources to strengthen professional communication and develop habits that can support both schoolwork and future business responsibilities.

This portfolio is a place to introduce my background and organize examples of my student learning. The projects described here reflect interests in writing, economics, environmental issues, performance, and technology. Building a resume website gives me another way to practice presenting information for an audience. I am at the beginning of my professional journey, so I focus on honest descriptions of what I have studied and what I want to improve. In the future, I hope to gain practical experience through internships, collaborative projects, and opportunities that connect business knowledge with useful digital tools and thoughtful communication.
